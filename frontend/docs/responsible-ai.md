# Responsible AI

ReLife Nexus is decision support for institutional assets, not autonomous procurement, disposal, hazardous repair, or financial approval.

- **Fairness:** recommendations use asset and request criteria, never department prestige or individual identity.
- **Transparency:** each result exposes confidence, reasons, evidence, assumptions, and review status.
- **Privacy:** the prototype minimizes personal information and does not expose private documents.
- **Uncertainty:** unknown values remain unknown; insufficient evidence produces `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- **Safety:** hazardous symptoms require professional inspection and human approval.
- **Auditability:** recommendations include timestamps, evidence references, and approval status.
